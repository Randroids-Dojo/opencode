Define the metrics for OpenCode-as-harness, including task success, patch quality, tool use, latency, cost, and released-build reliability.

The scorecard should separate:
- task completion rate
- patch acceptance or test-pass quality
- tool selection quality and misuse rate
- recovery quality after errors or dead ends
- latency, token, and cost efficiency
- released-build and installed-build pass rate
